Hederra Portfolio Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026 · Published by Abhiranjan Singh

Support and privacy: abhiranjansingh05@gmail.com

Who provides this app

Hederra Portfolio is an independent, read-only portfolio app provided by Abhiranjan Singh. It is not an official Hedera app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Hedera or CoinGecko. You do not create an account, connect a wallet, sign a transaction, or provide a private key or seed phrase. The app cannot move funds.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the last account address you submit, your manually entered purchase records, and your private address labels in a local SQLite database. Purchase records can include dates, HBAR quantities, costs, fees, currencies and source notes. These records support your journal and calculations. They are not automatically uploaded to the developer, a cloud account, or a synchronization service. Android app backup is disabled. On other platforms, operating-system backup and device-transfer behavior may depend on your device settings.

Public account and price requests

When you explore an account, refresh data, or load history, the app sends the public Hedera account address and relevant pagination, date or transaction-timestamp parameters over HTTPS to the public Hedera mirror-node service at mainnet-public.mirrornode.hedera.com. The service returns public ledger data, balances, transactions, rewards and historical exchange rates. Current HBAR price requests go to api.coingecko.com and include the asset identifier and requested currencies, not your purchase journal or private labels. Providers receive the IP address and technical request information needed to deliver their services and may retain logs under their own policies. Selecting an external transaction link opens that provider's website in your browser. The developer does not operate an account-data server for this Android release.

Imports, exports and sharing

The system file picker grants access only to files you choose for import. Imported purchase and label information is processed locally. Export or backup creates a file containing the records you choose to export; the operating system lets you choose where to save or share it. A backup can contain private financial information. Files you export or share are controlled by you and the destination service and are not removed when you clear the app's data.

Advertising, analytics and permissions

This release contains no advertising or analytics integration and does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, advertising-ID or broad photo/storage access. It uses internet access to retrieve public data. It does not sell your personal information or use your journal for advertising. Crash diagnostics you choose to send through Android or Google Play are governed by those services and your device settings.

Retention and deletion

Your saved address, purchases and labels stay on the device until you change or delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Use Forget saved account to clear the remembered address. In each account's Journal, delete individual purchases or use Clear this account’s local journal to remove that account's purchases and labels. Android Settings > Apps > Hederra Portfolio > Storage > Clear storage removes all local app data. Export a backup first if you want to keep your journal. Delete exported files separately from the locations or services where you saved them. Public blockchain transactions are not controlled by this app and cannot be deleted by its developer. Service-provider logs follow the providers' retention policies.

Security and international processing

Requests to the built-in data providers use HTTPS. Local app storage is protected by operating-system access controls, but the journal and exported backups are not separately password-encrypted by the app. Protect your device and backups. Data providers and any services you choose for sharing may process information in other countries. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Support, privacy requests and children

Contact Abhiranjan Singh at abhiranjansingh05@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. If you email us, your message, email address and any attachments are processed to respond; please do not send private keys, seed phrases or unnecessary financial records. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations; you can request deletion by email. The app is intended for adults and is not designed for children. There is no in-app registration or user account to delete.

Changes and data-provider policies

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected in the app and public policy. Current price data is provided by CoinGecko; public ledger data is supplied through Hedera mirror nodes. Review the linked provider policies for how they handle requests sent to them.
